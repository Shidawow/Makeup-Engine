# Internal Trial Evidence Collection Protocol

Phase 9F defines the preparation protocol for privacy-safe internal trial evidence collection. It is not a production data collection system, public recruitment, backend form, analytics pipeline, AI analysis workflow, training workflow, or production app approval.

## Allowed Anonymous Evidence

- Anonymous task completion notes.
- Anonymous step comprehension notes.
- Anonymous template value notes.
- Anonymous Shell usability notes.
- Anonymous recommendation usefulness notes.
- Anonymous privacy clarity notes.
- Anonymous trial operations notes.
- Aggregated issue counts.
- Aggregated severity summaries.
- Aggregated iteration priority summaries.

Allowed evidence must remain local, anonymous, and documented as observation or aggregate summary. It must not contain real names, contacts, photos, health information, sensitive identity information, biometric identifiers, account credentials, uploads, backend records, AI analysis records, or training data.

## Forbidden Data

Never collect real name, phone number, email, precise address, face photo, makeup photo, skin health information, health condition, sensitive identity, biometric identifier, face embedding, raw camera data, uploaded image, social media account, payment information, account credential, or training dataset write request.

## Participant Notice

Before any dry run, the participant-facing explanation must say: this is an internal anonymous local trial preparation flow; it does not collect photos, does not upload, does not train models, and does not collect sensitive or identifying information.

## Stop Conditions

Stop the session or stop recording immediately if anyone requests photos, camera access, upload, real identity, contact information, health information, sensitive identity, biometric data, AI automatic analysis, backend storage, or training usage.
