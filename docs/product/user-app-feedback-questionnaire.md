# User App Feedback Questionnaire

Phase 8C questionnaire for local MVP trial feedback.

This is a documented structure, not a backend form.

## Questions

1. Can you understand this makeup practice flow?
2. Would you be willing to follow these steps?
3. Does the number of steps feel too many, too few, or right?
4. Are tool and product suggestions useful?
5. Is the template recommendation helpful?
6. Is the local-only / no upload / no training privacy explanation clear?
7. Which step is most confusing?
8. Would you keep using this kind of guidance?
9. Would you pay for it or recommend it to a friend? This is a non-sensitive business interest question.
10. What else would you improve?

## Do Not Collect

Do not collect real names, contact information, photos, health information, sensitive identity information, face embeddings, biometric identifiers, backend records, analytics records, or training data.
