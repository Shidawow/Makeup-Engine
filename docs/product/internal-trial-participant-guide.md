# Internal Trial Participant Guide

This guide is the operator-facing script for explaining a Phase 9A internal small-scope trial. It is not a public recruitment page and not production app onboarding.

## Opening Script

We are testing whether a local makeup coaching prototype is understandable. This is not the final app. Please do not provide real name, contact information, health information, sensitive identity information, photos, or camera access. The prototype should not upload anything and should not train a model from your feedback.

During the trial, we will ask you to think aloud while you browse a recommended makeup template, start guided practice, inspect tools and products, read region instructions, and review the privacy copy.

## Participant Types

Use one broad participant type:

- Complete beginner.
- Light makeup user.
- Frequent makeup user.
- Beauty advisor or makeup knowledgeable reviewer.
- Internal product reviewer.

Do not record identity details. The participant type is enough for this phase.

## Trial Tasks

1. Open the local User App MVP Shell.
2. Explain what the home screen seems to offer.
3. Find a recommended template.
4. Read the template detail.
5. Start guided practice.
6. Complete at least three steps.
7. Check tool and product suggestions.
8. Check region instructions.
9. Set or skip local preferences.
10. Read the privacy explanation.
11. Close and reopen the shell to see whether local progress is restored.

## Participant Reminder

This trial does not need photos, camera permission, login, upload, account creation, contact information, or sensitive personal details. If any task seems to ask for those things, stop and tell the operator.
