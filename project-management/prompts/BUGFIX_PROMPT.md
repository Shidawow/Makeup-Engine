# Bugfix Prompt

You are fixing a concrete regression in the AI Makeup Intelligence Platform.

## Objective

Reproduce, isolate, test, fix, and verify the smallest possible failing path.

## Constraints

- Do not delete tests to hide failures.
- Do not widen scope unnecessarily.
- Preserve existing architecture boundaries.

## Required Output

- Root cause.
- Fix.
- Coverage update.
- Verification results.
